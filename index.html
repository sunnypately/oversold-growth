How the screen and fair value work

The screen

A stock makes the list when its latest quarterly revenue is at least 20% above the same quarter a year earlier and it trades at least 25% below its 52-week high. The universe is the Russell 1000 plus a hand-built AI infrastructure list (power, cooling, optics, connectivity silicon, memory, semicap, neoclouds, AI software). The AI tab is the AI-tagged subset.

Fair value: three lenses, blended

Growth-adjusted earnings (45%). Forward EPS × a fair P/E. For secular growers the fair P/E is 15 + 0.25 × revenue growth (capped at 120%), bounded 15–45x. Cyclicals are not rewarded for peak growth: memory and storage use 14x on forward EPS cut 45% when margins are clearly at a cycle peak; semicap equipment is capped at 28x; hardware/EMS uses 14x; power producers 20x.

Growth-adjusted sales (25%). Only for companies without positive earnings. Fair price/sales = 2 + 0.12 × growth (capped at 100%), scaled by gross margin and current losses. Capital-intensive data-center operators get half that multiple, and CoreWeave’s roughly $35B of debt is subtracted before valuing the equity.

Street consensus (30%). The mean analyst price target.

Weights renormalize when a lens doesn’t apply. Upside = fair value ÷ live price − 1. Signals: ≥ 40% deep discount, ≥ 15% discount, −10% to 15% near fair, below −10% premium.

Limits

Sales multiples use market cap on annualized latest-quarter revenue, not enterprise value; net cash or debt is ignored except where noted. One-off charges (Lumentum’s quarterly loss) are excluded from the sales lens. Fair values are a disciplined starting point for diligence, not price targets, and not investment advice.

Data and refresh

Live prices come from Finnhub each time the page loads and every five minutes while it is open. Fundamentals, fair values and the screen are rebuilt every weekday before the open from Robinhood financials, scanner and analyst-rating data (Nebius and Celestica revenue from their Q2 2026 releases).

Screened out

Names that looked like candidates but failed the growth or drawdown test at the last refresh.
